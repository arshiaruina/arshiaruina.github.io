## Volunteer - Communications, Operations & Engagement
[Climate Week Zurich](https://www.climateweekzurich.org)
(2026)

* Climate Week Zurich is a city-wide platform convening business, policy, and civil society to accelerate and scale measurable climate solutions

My responsibilities included:
* Ensured quality, consistency and coherence of partner event registrations with Climate Week Zurich guidelines
* Supported ambassador engagement across key events and on-site event operations, including participant registration
* Created social media content and edited website blogs to promote events and increase audience engagement
* Supported impact analysis by cleaning, standardising and consolidating participant data from partner events
